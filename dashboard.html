4. Cost-Carbon Pareto Front

Key insight: "Cheap" and "green" are not the same thing. The TOU and heuristic methods that chase cheap electricity end up burning more carbon than AFAP, because night-time valley electricity is cheap but often comes from coal baseload. The MILP co-optimizes both via a tunable carbon weight — the Pareto front above lets the operator choose their preferred trade-off.

5. Why We Trust These Results

  • Optimal baseline: MILP solved to proven optimality by CBC; the gap between our heuristic and MILP is the room for improvement.
  • Reproducibility: 10 random seeds averaged; no single-scenario cherry-picking.
  • Battery co-optimization: SOC dynamics, round-trip efficiency, and degradation cost are modeled explicitly (Hithium BESS parameters).
  • SLA preserved: zero deadline violations across all methods and scenarios.
